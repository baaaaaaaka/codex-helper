# v0.1.22-rc.33

## Why

The Teams local supervisor could mistake a live child that was waiting for
Graph/proxy initialization, or a fresh owner whose control-chat poll was
stalled, for a dead service. Repeated watchdog restarts then prevented the
service from retaining its durable backlog and recovering when connectivity
returned.

## What changed

- Treat a fresh local owner heartbeat as process liveness; a stalled control
  chat poll remains a degraded retry condition instead of restart evidence.
- Keep a verified local-supervisor child alive while it establishes its owner,
  including when Graph/proxy initialization lasts beyond the old fixed startup
  window.
- Preserve restart behavior for a genuinely stale current owner and reject
  unmanaged process identity mismatches.
- Add prolonged poll-outage, delayed-startup, owner-generation, and CI-selector
  regression coverage.

## Validation

- `go test -race -p 1 ./internal/cli ./internal/teams/store ./internal/teams -count=1`
- `go vet ./...`
- Docker real-process smoke
- Teams watchdog resource benchmark with no leaked goroutines or open stores
- `python3 -m unittest scripts/tests/test_ci_targeted_shards.py`
- `git diff --check`

## Compatibility

No transcript, checkpoint, outbox, ledger, or persisted watchdog-state format
changed. TUI-vs-Teams active-writer behavior is unchanged. The watchdog still
restarts a service when the current owner heartbeat is genuinely stale or
process identity cannot be verified; Graph/control-poll outages remain
in-process retry/degraded states.

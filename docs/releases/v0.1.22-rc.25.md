# v0.1.22-rc.25

## Why

Teams history polling and ownership recovery still had failure combinations
where one stalled Graph chat, control-chat backlog, parked chat, stale
continuation, or active inbound claim could delay unrelated chats or lose the
durable recovery frontier. These cases were especially visible after a helper
outage, during concurrent TUI activity, and at process takeover boundaries.

## What changed

- Bound and isolate per-chat Graph polling, reserve progress for work chats,
  and preserve fairness across control, hot, warm, parked, and continuation
  frontiers.
- Keep stale and multiple Graph continuation paths durable instead of silently
  dropping an unread frontier; advance cursors only when inbound ownership is
  still proven.
- Add claim tokens and compare-and-swap fencing so late poll completions,
  same-owner ABA handoffs, and deleted rows cannot overwrite a newer owner.
- Make history-watch startup preserve an unterminated live record while
  accepting valid JSON at EOF, and keep physical history progress independent
  from semantic publication.
- Add deterministic ownership pressure tests, strict CI selection checks, and
  isolated Docker coverage for Graph stalls, multi-day backlog recovery,
  TUI/Teams concurrency, parked-chat wakeup, stale continuation, process
  takeover, SIGKILL recovery, and SQLite filesystem-full retry behavior.

## Compatibility

The persisted state remains migration-aware and fail-closed for unknown legacy
mutations. Existing TUI-owned thread behavior is preserved: Teams does not
silently replay a request while another writer owns the Codex thread. Legacy
transcripts and ambiguous runtime ownership are not blindly rewritten or
replayed; they remain subject to the existing proof and operator recovery
paths. This prerelease should be canaried before broad rollout.

# v0.1.22-rc.10

## Why

Codex can reject an otherwise valid turn with `Selected model is at capacity. Please try a different model.` or an equivalent overloaded response. Treating that transient response as terminal interrupts the user's task.

## What changed

- Added bounded retries for transient model-capacity failures on the managed app-server runner, with ten consecutive retries by default.
- Reset the consecutive retry budget after substantive agent progress, while retaining a total retry and elapsed-time safety cap.
- Continue accepted turns with valid non-empty input, suppress transient failure events while retrying, and show a concise Teams retry status.
- Fail closed when a failed turn may already have produced command, tool, file, browser, or other side-effect items; preserve cancellation and same-thread turn serialization.
- Added classifier, retry-flow, side-effect, cancellation, Teams rendering, and regression test coverage.

## Validation

- `go test ./...`
- `go test -race ./internal/codexrunner -count=1 -timeout=120s`
- Targeted Teams retry-status race tests.
- `go vet ./internal/codexrunner ./internal/teams`
- `git diff --check`

## Compatibility

Retrying is limited to the managed `AppServerRunner` path used by the current Teams runtime. The legacy one-shot `ExecRunner` remains non-replaying because it has no accepted-turn continuation contract. No public protocol or persisted state format changes.

# v0.1.22-rc.24

## Why

Recent Teams chats could be blocked by transcript history recovery even when
there was no unresolved live Codex execution. Large, opaque, malformed, or
partial transcript records could also keep the physical history cursor from
advancing, making later status and final messages unreachable.

## What changed

- Separate physical transcript progress, history-only quarantine, semantic
  delivery state, and live execution ownership.
- Persist source generation, context-gap proof, terminal-only boundaries, and
  bounded pending history ranges through the existing checkpoint/store
  transactions with monotonic observation checks.
- Keep history recovery from creating a runtime owner or blocking new live
  Teams requests; preserve fail-closed behavior for genuinely unknown runtime
  ownership and legacy state.
- Make malformed, opaque, oversized, and newline-less partial records
  recoverable across polls without livelock, while keeping bounded scan and
  history-outbox budgets.
- Prevent normal mixed-ID final mirrors from being classified as orphan
  continuations, and add regression coverage for the affected session shapes,
  JSON/SQLite parity, and Graph no-replay behavior.

## Validation

- `go test ./... -count=1`
- `go test -race ./internal/teams ./internal/teams/store -count=1`
- `go vet ./...`
- `git diff --check`
- Same-machine before/after history and SQLite benchmark comparison recorded in
  `docs/teams-history-safe-recovery-checklist.md`.

## Compatibility

The change preserves existing JSON/SQLite checkpoint formats and treats legacy
unknown ownership conservatively. It does not automatically migrate ambiguous
legacy runtime rows, replay non-idempotent Graph sends, or modify live Teams
state. The prerelease should be canaried before broad rollout; no Teams helper
was restarted as part of this release preparation.
